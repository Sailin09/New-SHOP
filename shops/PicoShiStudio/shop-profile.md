## Current Product Direction

## Visual Direction
- Clean, warm, neutral, premium but not overly luxurious.
- Natural wood, cream, beige, light gray, taupe, walnut-brown wardrobe accents.
- Soft natural light.
- Minimalist closet / wardrobe styling.
- Product must remain the visual focus.
- Avoid busy props and unrelated decor.

## Image Output Preference
- Etsy-ready images should normally be 1:1.
- Final images should not contain internal workflow numbers.
- Product-truth images should prioritize exact real-product structure.
- Lifestyle scenes may change only the environment, not the hanger design.

## Listing Tone
- Clear, practical, elegant.
- Emphasize structure, dimensions, use, craftsmanship, and wardrobe organization.
- Avoid unsupported claims.
- Unknown facts must be marked TBD.

## Core Rule
**Product accuracy first. Scene styling second. Product Facts = source of truth.**
