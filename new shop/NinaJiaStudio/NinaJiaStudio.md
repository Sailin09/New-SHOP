# NinaJiaStudio｜Etsy 风格包包图片模板

## 固定视觉与真实产品约束
- 输出 7 张 1:1 正方形图片，按 Etsy 商品图浏览顺序排列。
- 延续参考图的干净米白背景、柔和自然窗光和低干扰构图；每张图承担一种清晰的信息功能。
- 所有生成均以同一张真实产品参考图为准，严格保持包体轮廓、纹样、颜色、比例、缝线、材质外观、五金和连接点。
- 产品只有一根连续肩带，从左右上方连接点形成一个 U 形；不得添加第二根肩带、额外提手、背带、内袋、五金、材质、尺寸或功能。
- 不生成品牌 Logo、水印、UI 或未经确认的配色。尺寸未知时留空，由人工填写。

## 通用提示词
将 [真实产品参考图] 替换为实际产品图片，逐图生成。每张都必须输出一张 1:1 正方形图片，并加入：保持真实产品结构；只有一根连续肩带；不添加未确认部件、材质、尺寸、功能或配色；无文字、Logo、水印。

### 1｜主图
Create a clean Etsy hero photograph based on [真实产品参考图]. Show exactly one complete bag, centered and fully visible, on a warm ivory wall and pale stone surface with soft natural window light. Preserve exact silhouette, motif, color, proportions, seams, hardware, and the one continuous U-shaped shoulder strap. No extra handles, straps, pockets, hardware, text, logo, or watermark. Output one square 1:1 image.

### 2｜面料/纹样细节
Create a square 1:1 close-up of the exact same bag, focusing only on visible fabric weave, motif, stitching, edge finish, and real strap attachment. Preserve scale and palette. Do not invent microscopic detail, material, or construction. Warm ivory setting, soft light, no text or watermark.

### 3｜开口/内部结构
Create a square 1:1 image of the exact same bag opened only as far as the real product allows. Show only the real opening, lining, closure, and interior features visible in [真实产品参考图]. Do not invent pockets, compartments, closures, contents, or accessories. Preserve the single-strap structure.

### 4｜尺寸信息版
Create a square 1:1 front image of the exact same bag with subtle empty guide lines and blank label boxes for width, height, and depth. Leave all values empty for manual entry. Do not add numbers, specifications, or functional claims.

### 5｜平铺/俯拍
Create a square 1:1 top-down flat-lay of the exact same bag and its single continuous shoulder strap, fully visible on a warm ivory surface. Preserve the true shape, pattern, color, and connection points. No extra products or invented accessories.

### 6｜场景陈列
Create a square 1:1 lifestyle display photograph of exactly one confirmed bag resting on a simple table, chair, or shelf in a warm ivory interior with soft window light. Keep the bag unobstructed and preserve every real product detail. No extra bags, props that imply unconfirmed function, text, logo, or watermark.

### 7｜上身效果
Create a square 1:1 lifestyle photograph of a person wearing the exact same bag with its one continuous shoulder strap over one shoulder. Keep the bag unobstructed and clearly visible; use a simple neutral outfit and no face focus. Preserve exact color, motif, silhouette, hardware, and strap construction. No extra bags, text, logo, or watermark.

## 使用前核对
1. 七张图是否来自同一真实产品及已确认配色？
2. 是否全部为 1:1，且顺序为主图、细节、内部、尺寸、平铺、陈列、上身？
3. 是否始终只有一根连续肩带？
4. 是否误加了内袋、五金、材质、尺寸、功能或未确认配色？
5. 尺寸信息是否留空待人工填写？
