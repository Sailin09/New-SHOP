# NinaJiaStudio｜包类产品图上架模板

## 固定视觉规范

- 输出 **7 张图**，全部为 **1:1 正方形**。
- 延续参考图的简约、干净、米白背景与柔和自然窗光。
- 使用真实产品参考图；产品结构、纹样、颜色、比例、五金和肩带连接方式必须保持一致。
- 本产品约束：**只有一根连续肩带**，从左右上方连接点形成一个 U 形；不得生成第二根带子、额外提手、背带、内袋或不存在的五金。
- 不生成品牌 Logo、水印、UI、无依据的尺寸/材质/性能文字。
- 尺寸图只保留空白标注框，实际数值由上架前人工填写。

## 通用提示词

将 `[真实产品参考图]` 替换为实际产品图片，并逐图生成。每一张都必须加入：`输出一张 1:1 正方形图片`。

### 1｜主图

```text
Create a clean ecommerce hero photograph based on [真实产品参考图]. Show exactly one complete bag, centered and fully visible, on a warm ivory wall and pale stone surface with soft natural window light and faint leaf shadows. Preserve the exact bag silhouette, fabric pattern, colors, seams, hardware, proportions, and attachment points from the reference. The product has exactly one continuous shoulder strap connecting the left and right upper attachment points and hanging in one U across the front; do not add a second strap, extra handles, loops, or hardware. Minimal background, no text, no logo, no watermark. Output one square 1:1 image.
```

### 2｜容量/内部

```text
Create a square 1:1 ecommerce image of the exact same bag from [真实产品参考图], opened only as far as the real product allows, showing realistic everyday contents arranged neatly. Preserve the original single-strap construction and all visible product details. Do not invent pockets, lining, closures, materials, or accessories that are not visible in the reference. Warm ivory background, soft daylight, clean composition, no text or watermark.
```

### 3｜面料与工艺

```text
Create a square 1:1 close product photograph of the exact same bag from [真实产品参考图], focusing on the visible fabric weave, printed motif, edge finishing, stitching, and real strap attachment. Preserve the original colors, motif scale, hardware, and one-strap structure. Do not redesign or add parts. Minimal ivory setting, soft natural light, no text, logo, or watermark.
```

### 4｜尺寸图

```text
Create a square 1:1 front product image of the exact same bag from [真实产品参考图]. Add only subtle empty measurement guide lines for width and body height outside the silhouette; leave all label boxes blank for later manual values. Preserve the exact one-strap design and product proportions. No readable numbers, no invented specifications, no text, no watermark.
```

### 5｜上身图

```text
Create a square 1:1 lifestyle photograph of a person wearing the exact same bag from [真实产品参考图] with its one continuous shoulder strap over one shoulder. Keep the bag unobstructed and clearly visible. Preserve exact color, motif, silhouette, hardware, and strap construction. Use a simple cream outfit, warm ivory interior, and soft window light. No face focus, no extra bags, no text, no watermark.
```

### 6｜纹样近景

```text
Create a square 1:1 macro detail photograph of the actual visible fabric and motif from [真实产品参考图]. Show the real weave, print/embroidery appearance, stitching, and edge texture without inventing microscopic detail or changing colors. Keep the reference pattern scale and palette. Warm ivory background, soft light, no text or watermark.
```

### 7｜配色/陈列图

```text
Create a square 1:1 ecommerce image using [真实产品参考图]. If multiple confirmed colorways exist, show only those exact colorways with identical construction and exactly one continuous shoulder strap per bag. If no confirmed colorways exist, show one bag in a wider clean composition. Never recolor by guesswork or invent additional products. Warm ivory background, soft natural light, no text, logo, or watermark.
```

## 使用前核对

1. 所有来源图是否为同一真实产品及已确认配色。
2. 七张图是否全部为 1:1。
3. 是否始终只有一根连续肩带。
4. 是否误加了内袋、五金、尺寸、材质或性能描述。
5. 缩略图下产品是否完整、清晰、构图有区分度。
